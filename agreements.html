1. PURPOSE & INTENT

This binding agreement is made between the parties above to connect and govern the real estate project described as: Plot 84, Block 214, Kololo Hill Luxury Villa Development. The parties mutually agree to fulfill all covenants, quality standards, and ethical codes set forth in this instrument.

2. CONSIDERATION, FEES & PAYMENT MILESTONES

The total agreed consideration for the performance of this agreement is UGX 450,000,000 (Four Hundred Fifty Million UGX). Payment shall be executed in staggered milestone tranches:
• Phase 1 (Site Prep & Substructure): 25% upon completion of excavation and foundation casting.
• Phase 2 (Superstructure & Roofing): 35% upon structural framing, brickwork, and roof beam lock.
• Phase 3 (Finishing, MEP & Handover): 30% upon tiling, electrical, plumbing, and painting.
• Phase 4 (Retention Escrow): 10% retained for a 90-day defect liability warranty period.

3. COVENANTS & PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

(a) The Contractor guarantees that all materials and workmanship comply strictly with Uganda National Bureau of Standards (UNBS) and international architectural specifications.
(b) The Client agrees to provide unhindered site access and approve certified milestone valuations within 5 business days of architect sign-off.
(c) Any modification or variation in scope must be documented in writing and approved in the EOTO Connectivity Portal.

4. GOVERNING LAW & DIGITAL AUTHENTICITY

This instrument is governed by the laws of Uganda and the East African Community. The parties agree that digital signatures applied through the EOTO Properties platform carry full legal equivalence to ink signatures under the Electronic Transactions Act.
